# Dragon Ball Z - Attack of the Saiyans

## Status

This world is an early work in progress. It only covers the prologue (Training Island) and is not playable yet:
the game client is still being written.

## What does randomization do to this game?

The items found in chests are shuffled into the multiworld. Opening a chest sends a check; items received from
other players are added directly to your inventory.

## What is the goal?

Defeat Jackie Chun at the end of the prologue. The final goal will be to defeat Vegeta once the whole story is
supported.

## Which locations are randomized?

The eight chests of Training Island.

## Which items can be found?

The original contents of those chests: Small Beans, a Medium Bean, an HP Capsule 50, a Phoenix Feather, the Gas
Mask and the Darkness Card.

## Supported version

Only the European release (game code `BRPP`, revision 0) is supported. It has only been tested in French.
